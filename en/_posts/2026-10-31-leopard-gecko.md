The leopard gecko (Eublepharis macularius) lives in the arid regions of Afghanistan, Pakistan, India and Iran. It is one of the most commonly kept reptiles because it is calm and hardy.

## Appearance
About 20-25 cm long, with a yellow body spotted in dark brown, like a leopard. Unlike many geckos it has movable eyelids and cannot climb glass. It stores fat in its tail: a thick tail means a healthy animal.

## Enclosure
An adult does well in a terrarium of at least 60x45x45 cm. It needs a warm side at 30-32 °C (heat mat or lamp) and a cooler one at 24-26 °C, plus a humid hide for shedding. Avoid loose sand as substrate. It is crepuscular and nocturnal. Keep only one male per enclosure.

## Diet
Insectivore: crickets, mealworms and waxworms, dusted with calcium and vitamin D3. Adults eat every 2-3 days. In captivity it lives 15-20 years.
