The Arabian horse comes from the Arabian Peninsula, where the Bedouin bred it for stamina and closeness to people. It is one of the oldest horse breeds and helped shape many others, including the Thoroughbred.

## Appearance
Standing 145-156 cm at the withers, it has a small head with a dished profile, large eyes, an arched neck and a tail carried high in motion. The most common colours are grey, bay, chestnut and black.

## Temperament
Spirited, intelligent and very sensitive. It bonds with people and learns fast, but needs a calm, consistent rider.

## Use
It excels at endurance racing, where stamina is decisive, and is also used for pleasure riding and showing. It is a long-lived horse, often past 25 years.

## Health
Some genetic diseases, such as severe combined immunodeficiency (SCID) and cerebellar abiotrophy, can be detected by testing breeding animals: always ask for the results before buying.
