The domestic canary descends from the wild canary of the Canary Islands, Madeira and the Azores. Bred in Europe since the 16th century, it is prized for its song and colours.

## Appearance
It measures 12-14 cm. There are colour varieties (yellow, red, white, brown), type varieties (such as the crested Gloster) and song varieties (such as the Harzer Roller and the Timbrado). It is mostly the male that sings.

## Diet
A base of canary seed mix (canary grass, rapeseed, niger), plus fresh greens, a little fruit and a cuttlebone for calcium. Change the water daily.

## Cage and care
It needs a cage wider than it is tall so it can fly horizontally, with perches of different thickness and a bath. Keep it in a bright room, away from drafts and from the kitchen: fumes from non-stick pans are toxic to birds. Males are territorial, so normally one canary per cage. In captivity it lives 8-10 years on average, sometimes up to 15.
