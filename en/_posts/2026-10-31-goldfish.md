The goldfish (Carassius auratus) comes from China, where it has been bred for over a thousand years. It lives 10-15 years and, with good care, even longer.

## Varieties
Long-bodied forms (common, comet, shubunkin) can exceed 25-30 cm and suit a pond. Round-bodied forms (oranda, telescope eye, lionhead) stay smaller but are more delicate.

## Aquarium
A round bowl is not suitable: it needs a spacious tank, at least 100 litres for the first fish and about 40 litres more for each additional one. It produces a lot of waste, so you need a strong filter and regular water changes. Before adding fish, the tank must complete the nitrogen cycle. It prefers cool water, 18-22 °C, with no heater.

## Diet
Omnivore: good flakes or pellets, plus shelled peas and greens. Feed small amounts, because it fouls the water and gets fat easily.
