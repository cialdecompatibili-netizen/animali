Demo article. Choosing kibble is not about the brand: what matters is that it suits the dog.

## Size
A small dog needs smaller kibble that is richer in energy per kilo. A large dog needs larger kibble and a controlled calorie intake to protect the joints.

## Age
Puppies, adults and seniors have different needs. Puppies need more protein and calcium to grow, seniors fewer calories and more fibre.

## Activity
A working or very sporty dog burns more than a couch dog. If weight goes up, reduce the portion without changing food right away.

## How to switch food
The change should take 7-10 days, mixing increasing amounts of the new food into the old one. For dogs with allergies, digestive problems or illness, ask the vet first.
