The Maine Coon comes from the state of Maine (USA) and is one of the largest domestic cat breeds. It is nicknamed the "gentle giant" for its easygoing nature.

## Appearance
Males usually weigh 5.5-8 kg and females 3.5-5.5 kg, reaching full size slowly, between 3 and 5 years. The coat is semi-long, dense and water-resistant, with a chest ruff, ear tufts and a bushy tail. It comes in many colours, including the classic brown tabby.

## Temperament
Sociable, playful and people-oriented, it gets along with children, dogs and other cats. It talks with trills and chirps rather than loud meows.

## Care and health
Brush the coat 2-3 times a week to prevent tangles. It needs space, sturdy scratching posts and daily play. It is prone to hypertrophic cardiomyopathy and hip dysplasia: choose catteries that run genetic tests and heart scans. Life expectancy is 12-15 years.
