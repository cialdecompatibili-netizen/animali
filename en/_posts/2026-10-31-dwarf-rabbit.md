The dwarf rabbit is a small-sized variety of the domestic rabbit (Oryctolagus cuniculus). It usually weighs 1-2 kg and lives 8-12 years.

## Temperament
Curious, social and clean: it can learn to use a litter tray. It is not a cage animal: it needs to run and jump for several hours a day, in a pen or a rabbit-proofed room (cables and toxic plants out of reach).

## Diet
The foundation is hay, always available: it wears down the teeth, which grow throughout life. Add fresh vegetables and a small portion of pellets. Fruit and carrots only as treats, because they are sugary.

## Company and health
Rabbits are social: a neutered pair is best. Vaccines against myxomatosis and rabbit haemorrhagic disease (RHD) should be discussed with a vet experienced in exotic pets. Warning signs: loss of appetite, small or no droppings, overgrown teeth.
